## Prompt panorámico

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: high-quality landscape photograph used as the background behind a Bible web app's hero and reading selector. Create a brand new inspiring, serene alpine valley landscape with natural believable photographic detail, snow-dusted rugged mountain peaks, layered distant mist, evergreen woodland on gently rolling hills and a subtle winding river/valley through the center. Wide landscape composition ideally 3840x2160, suitable for both a panoramic desktop view and a narrow central mobile crop. Mountains and woodland must occupy most of the frame and remain visible even in a central portrait crop; sky is only the upper 20-25%, never a big empty blue field. Place textured peaks from upper quarter to middle, valley slopes and trees throughout lower half. Soft warm early morning light from the upper right, muted pale gold, slate blue, natural forest green; restrained saturation and realistic atmosphere. The left upper and middle areas need calm low-contrast softly misted mountain layers with even pale warm illumination to support dark text, while the right has more defined mountain/forest detail. No glaring sun disk, no blown white haze, no artificial vignette, no black borders. Keep landscape definition and gentle tonal transitions throughout; website will add its own subtle theme-aware gradient. No people, buildings, religious figures, crosses, books, logos, captions, words, UI, watermark, illustration or painterly effects. This must be the landscape asset alone, not a website mockup.
